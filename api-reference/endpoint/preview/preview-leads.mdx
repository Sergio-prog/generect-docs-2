## Use Case:
Show masked lead results in SaaS products without exposing paid data such as email, phone, LinkedIn URL, or company domain.

## Pricing
This endpoint is billed at **$0.002 per returned result** and becomes **free once your account passes $2,000 total spend** (check the `preview_tier` flag on [`GET /accounts/me/`](/api-reference/endpoint/accounts/get-account-me)). Sizing the audience with `POST /preview/leads/count/` is always free. See [Pricing](/billing/pricing).
