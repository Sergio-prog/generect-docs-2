## Use Case:
Retrieve the current account profile, credit balance, monthly usage, and Preview tier eligibility.

<Note>`preview_tier` is `true` while [`POST /preview/leads/`](/api-reference/endpoint/preview/preview-leads) is free for you — that is, once your spend in the **current calendar month** reaches \$2,000. It flips back to `false` on the 1st of each month.</Note>
